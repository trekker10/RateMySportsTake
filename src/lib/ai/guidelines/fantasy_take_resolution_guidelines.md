# Take Resolution Guidelines — Fantasy Football Takes

## Step 1 — Identify Time Horizon from Language

| Language in Take | Horizon Type |
|---|---|
| "this week", "in Week X", "start him Sunday", "sit him" | Weekly |
| "this season", "in 2026", "rest of year", "ROS" | Season-long |
| "next season", "in 2027", "redraft next year" | Next season |
| "dynasty", "long-term hold", "next 3 years" | Dynasty / multi-year |
| "never draft", "always a bust", "career backup" | Career / dynasty |

---

## Step 2 — Resolution Dates by Fantasy Horizon

| Horizon | Resolution Date | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Weekly start/sit | **End of that NFL game week** | Sunday night or Monday for MNF |
| Waiver wire add | **End of that week** | Did he produce when added? |
| Season-long projection | **Jan 7** (year+1) | After fantasy championship week |
| Preseason / redraft take about current season | **Jan 7** (same year+1) | End of fantasy playoffs |
| Redraft take made in offseason about next season | **Jan 7** (year+2) | Full season out |
| Dynasty / multi-year | **Jan 7** (year+3 or +4) | Judgment call based on language |
| Career / "never draft again" | Age-based (see Step 3) | |

**Key rule:** Always resolve to the **fantasy championship week**, not the NFL regular season end. Fantasy playoffs run through Week 17, championship in Week 17/18 — resolution is after that.

---

## Step 3 — Career/Dynasty Takes: Age-Based Estimation

Fantasy value decays faster than real-world value — use tighter windows:

| Player Age | Fantasy Career Window | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| 22–24 | Through ~30 | RBs especially short |
| 25–27 | Through ~30–31 | Prime window closing |
| 28–29 | Through ~31–32 | 2–3 seasons max |
| 30+ | Through ~32–33 | Likely 1–2 seasons |

### Position-Specific Fantasy Value Windows
- RB: fantasy-relevant through ~29–30 (workload drops fast)
- WR: fantasy-relevant through ~33–34
- TE: fantasy-relevant through ~34–35 (Kelce effect)
- QB: fantasy-relevant through ~37–38

---

## Step 4 — Fantasy-Specific Edge Cases

**ADP / draft ranking takes** — resolve at end of the draft season they reference. If a take says "X is being drafted too high in 2026 drafts," resolution is Jan 7, 2027.

**Injury / usage takes** ("X will be the lead back once Y returns") — resolve at end of that season OR when the usage situation clarifies, whichever is first.

**Trade advice** ("sell high on X") — resolve at end of that season. Did selling high prove correct based on how X finished?

**Breakout / bust calls** — always season-long, resolve Jan 7 of that season's end.

**Dynasty rookie takes** ("X is a dynasty must-have") — use multi-year window, Jan 7 three seasons out as default.

---

## Quick Decision Tree

```
Weekly advice (start/sit/waiver/matchup)?
  → End of that game week

Season-long projection or breakout/bust call?
  → Jan 7 of that season's end (e.g. Jan 7, 2027 for 2026 season)

Offseason redraft take about upcoming season?
  → Jan 7 of that upcoming season's end

Dynasty or "long-term" language?
  → Jan 7, three seasons out (adjust based on player age)

"Never draft" / "always avoid" / career bust?
  → Age-based: RB cap at 30, WR at 34, TE at 35

ADP or draft value take?
  → Jan 7 of the season those drafts feed into

None of the above?
  → Default to Jan 7 of current season's end
```
